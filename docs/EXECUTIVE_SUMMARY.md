Current Artificial Intelligence architectures rely on brute-force tensor matrix multiplication on GPUs. This approach faces severe physical limits: thermal throttling, massive energy consumption, hardware supply bottlenecks, and prohibitive costs for scaling multi-agent systems.

## 2. THE SOLUTION: RESONANT COMPUTING
Nexalynk introduces a paradigm shift: replacing raw matrix arithmetic with hybrid wave mechanics. By combining digital square-wave timing with continuous sinusoidal carriers inside a simulated non-linear magnetic chamber, Nexalynk achieves:
* Real-time synchronization across autonomous sub-brains.
* Built-in signal opacity and physical-layer obfuscation.
* Minimal memory and energy footprint running natively on CPU architectures.
